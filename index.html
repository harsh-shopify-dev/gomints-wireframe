What exactly is Go Mints?
Go Mints is a caffeine-infused energy mint made with natural caffeine, L-Theanine, and Vitamin B6 & B12. Each mint delivers clean, focused energy — no coffee, no cans, no crash. Just pop one and feel it kick in under 4 minutes.
How is this different from energy drinks or coffee?
Unlike coffee or energy drinks, Go Mints has zero sugar, zero calories, and fits in your pocket. The combination of natural caffeine + L-Theanine gives you energy without the jitters or the 3 PM crash. No brewing, no spilling, no waiting.
What flavours are available?
We currently offer Coffee flavour — designed to give you that familiar coffee kick in mint form. More flavours are on the way. Stay tuned.
Is Go Mints vegetarian?
Yes, 100%. Go Mints is completely vegetarian, gluten-free, and contains no artificial colors or preservatives.
How many mints can I have in a day?
We recommend up to 3 mints per day. Each mint contains around 50mg of natural caffeine — roughly half a cup of coffee. Space them out as needed through your day for steady, sustained energy.
How fast does it kick in?
Most people feel the effect in under 4 minutes. The mint dissolves on your tongue, and the caffeine is absorbed directly — bypassing the slow digestion route of coffee or energy drinks.
Can I take it before a workout?
Absolutely. Pop one 5–10 minutes before your session. The natural caffeine + L-Theanine combo helps with alertness and focus without a heavy stomach or sugar crash mid-set.
Is it safe for daily use?
Yes. Go Mints is a nutraceutical product made with well-researched, food-grade ingredients. As with any caffeinated product, stay within the recommended daily intake and consult a doctor if you're pregnant, nursing, or have caffeine sensitivity.
